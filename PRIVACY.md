Privacy Policy for Bookmark Bar Switcher

Effective Date: 21 Juli 2025

Bookmark Bar Switcher is committed to protecting your privacy. This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or user data.

Information Collection
This extension does not collect any of the following:

Personal information (such as name, email, etc.)

Browsing history

Usage data

Cookies or tracking data

Data Usage
The extension operates entirely within your local browser environment. It uses the Chrome bookmarks API to read and modify your bookmarks, and the storage API to locally store preferences such as the order of your bookmark configurations. All data stays within your browser and is never sent or shared externally.

Third Parties
Bookmark Bar Switcher does not share any data with third-party services, servers, or external APIs. It does not use analytics, advertising networks, or trackers.

Changes to This Policy
This privacy policy may be updated in the future. Any changes will be reflected here with an updated effective date.

If you have any questions or concerns about this policy, feel free to contact the developer via the Chrome Web Store support link.

By using Bookmark Bar Switcher, you acknowledge and agree to this privacy policy.
